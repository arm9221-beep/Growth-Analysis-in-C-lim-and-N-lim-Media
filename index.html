Summary

Growth assays were run for 72 hours rather than the standard 24, because slow growth was anticipated in the nutrient-limited C-lim and Gln-lim (N-lim) media.

The FLC concentrations tested were changed between the two assays: the July 9 assay used a lower range (2–32 μg/mL), but because there was not a clear dropoff in effectiveness as the concentration increased, the July 14 assay shifted to a higher range (8–128 μg/mL) to better capture the dose-response curve.

Dose-response method adapted from the Babraham Bioinformatics IC50 tutorial.
